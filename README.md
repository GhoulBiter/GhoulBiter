# Mike Hanna

**ML Engineer · Agentic AI · Technical Lead**

I build AI systems for real operational problems: retrieval, agentic workflows, evaluation, model-serving, and backend infrastructure.

My work sits at the intersection of product, engineering, and applied AI. I care most about systems that make people’s work easier, reduce operational friction, and turn complex processes into usable software.

Currently, I lead AI/chat engineering at Rihal on enterprise knowledge-base systems for government and private-sector clients. I also co-founded Walkk, where I lead product and engineering across navigation, indoor route optimization, real-time analytics, incident reporting, and operational intelligence.
